Who we are

More than a society.
A family on campus.

The Muslim Students' Society of Nigeria, AATU chapter, exists to help every Muslim student grow in faith, excel in learning and serve the community with character.

From weekly halaqah and Jumu'ah coordination to academic support, welfare and campus-wide programmes, we build a space where faith and scholarship meet.
