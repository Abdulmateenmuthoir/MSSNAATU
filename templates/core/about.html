About the society

Rooted in faith.
Building for the future.

The Muslim Students' Society of Nigeria (MSSN), AATU chapter, is a community of Muslim students committed to worshipping Allah, pursuing knowledge and serving the campus with integrity.

Our story

A society built around people.

MSSN AATU exists to be a home for Muslim students at Abiola Ajimobi Technical University, Ibadan. We are part of the national Muslim Students' Society of Nigeria — a body with a long tradition of da'wah, education and student welfare.

On campus, our work is practical. We coordinate Jumu'ah and daily prayers, run halaqah and study circles, support students academically, organise programmes that strengthen faith and brotherhood, and stand with members in times of need.
