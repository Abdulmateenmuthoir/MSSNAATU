Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about membership, programmes and student services.

What is MSSN AATU?+

MSSN AATU is the Abiola Ajimobi Technical University chapter of the Muslim Students’ Society of Nigeria. We support students’ spiritual growth, academic life, welfare and service on campus.

How do I join the society?+

Muslim students can attend our announced programmes and contact the PRO office for help connecting with the society and its committees.

How can I get my member portal login?+

The PRO office creates member profiles and sends login details to the email address on file. If you have not received yours, contact the office.

Where can I find events and society updates?+

Upcoming programmes appear on the programmes page. News and articles are in the articles section.

Can students comment on articles?+

Yes. Sign in to your member account, open an article and add your comment below the story. Comments should be respectful and relevant.

How do I access past questions?+

Open the library to browse available resources by faculty, department and level. Sign in where member access is requested.
